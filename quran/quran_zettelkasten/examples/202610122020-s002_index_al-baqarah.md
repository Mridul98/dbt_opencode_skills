# Surah 2: THE HEIFER (al-Baqarah)
**UID:** 202610122020
**Type:** Index / Surah Map
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-13

## 1. Orientation
* **Revelation:** Medinan — the first long surah revealed after the Hijrah (classical consensus; a minority of transmitted reports attach individual verses, e.g., the final pair `[2:285–286]`, to later moments).
* **Size:** 286 ayahs, 40 ruku — the longest surah; sits at revelation order 87.
* **Central theme:** Building the believing community (ummah) out of a recited Book: it answers the Fatihah's petition by describing the guided (1–7), sorts humanity by its response (creeds and hypocrites), retells Israel's covenant history as mirror and warning (40–141), reclaims Abraham's house and religion for the monotheists (122–141), executes the decisive turn from Jerusalem's direction to the Ka'bah (142–152), and then legislates the life of the new nation — food, prayer, fasting, charity, marriage, divorce, debt, commerce, warfare (168–283) — closing on the creedal handshake of 285–286.

## 3. Structural Arc
* **Creed & sorting (1–20):** the Book's self-declaration and the first census of hearts — guided, sealed, pretenders. Answers `[1:6–7]` directly (see Knowledge note on guidance).
* **Origin stories (21–39):** worship → Adam → humanity's status as successors; the surah's anthropology before its sociology.
* **Israel mirror (40–141):** covenant, breach, calf, cow, killed prophets — the older community as warning for the new one; the "People of the Book" argument climaxed in the Abrahamic reclamation.
* **The turn (142–152):** qiblah, remembrance, martyrdom's frame — the community gets its geography and its posture.
* **Law-as-formation (153–283):** patience, food, righteousness, fasting, Hajj, fighting, property, women, debt — legislation given as belonging, repeatedly bracketed by "Allah knows what you do."
* **Handshake (284–286):** total sovereignty answered by total submission; "Allah does not burden any soul beyond its scope" closes where the Fatihah's petition began.
